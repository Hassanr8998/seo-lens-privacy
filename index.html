SEO Lens Privacy Policy

Applies to the SEO Lens Chrome extension, version 1.0.1 and later. Last updated: October 21, 2026.

Summary

SEO Lens collects no data. It has no account or login. It does not use analytics or tracking, does not log anything remotely, and has no server of its own. Nothing you browse or audit is sent to the developer or sold to or shared with anyone.

What the extension does

  • SEO Lens reads a tab only after you open the SEO Lens side panel on it. While the panel is open, switching tabs runs the audit on the new tab.
  • The audit runs locally in your browser on the page you are viewing. It reads headings, links, images, meta tags, structured data and social tags. Results stay in the panel. They leave your computer only when you choose to export a CSV or JSON file to your own device.
  • Your light/dark theme choice is saved in chrome.storage on your computer. Nothing else is stored.

Network requests

SEO Lens makes network requests only for checks you start yourself, or after you have granted optional site access:

  • Broken-link checks, image file sizes, HTTP status, redirect chain, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and indexability of linked pages.

Those requests go straight from your browser to the websites being checked. They do not pass through any SEO Lens server, and the extension does not keep the results after you close the panel.

Permissions and why each is needed

PermissionWhy it is needed
activeTabLets SEO Lens read the tab you opened the panel on, and only that tab, so it can audit it.
scriptingRuns the audit code in that tab so it can read the rendered page (headings, links, images, meta tags, schema).
storageSaves your light/dark theme on your computer.
sidePanelShows the audit in Chrome's side panel next to the page.
Optional host access (http://*/*, https://*/*)Requested only when you first start a network check (broken links, file sizes, status, redirects, robots.txt, sitemap, indexability). It lets the extension request those URLs. You can decline or revoke it, and the on-page audit keeps working.
Optional webRequestRecords each redirect hop (status code and location) for the extension's own status check request. It does not monitor your browsing. If you decline it, the panel shows only the final URL.

You can revoke optional permissions at any time from chrome://extensions → SEO Lens → Details.

Third parties and children

SEO Lens contains no third-party SDKs, ads or trackers, and it loads no remote code. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Any future feature that collects data would be disclosed here and in the Chrome Web Store listing before it ships.
